## Name, pronoun, and gender change

Roosevelt honors student requests to use a preferred name or change a gender designation or
pronouns, subject to restrictions on inappropriate requests. Submit a request through
[Update Personal Information](https://www.roosevelt.edu/current-students/academics/grades-transcripts/update-personal-info).

## Veterans services

Veterans Services supports veterans and active service members as they transition into the
university community. Contact the Office of Veterans Services at ovs@roosevelt.edu.
