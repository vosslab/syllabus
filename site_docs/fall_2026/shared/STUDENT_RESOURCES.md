# Help and student services

These shared Fall 2026 pages organize university services by the task a student is trying to
complete. Every complete syllabus download includes the overview and each topic page once.

## Find the right help

- [Plan courses and manage records](student_services/ACADEMIC_ADVISING.md): academic advising,
  department advising, registration, transcripts, credit evaluation, and graduation.
- [Get help with learning or research](student_services/LEARNING_SUPPORT.md): tutoring, coaching,
  libraries, study groups, laboratory support, the STEM Center, and English language support.
- [Explore support programs and careers](student_services/PROGRAMS_AND_CAREER.md): TRIO Student
  Support Services, McNair Scholars, internships, employment, and graduate-school planning.
- [Use technology and join campus life](student_services/TECHNOLOGY_AND_CAMPUS.md): technology help,
  Wi-Fi, university apps, student organizations, events, and athletics.
- [Pay for school and meet essential needs](student_services/MONEY_AND_ESSENTIAL_NEEDS.md): financial
  aid, emergency grants, and food or hygiene support.
- [Find health, identity, and safety support](student_services/HEALTH_IDENTITY_AND_SAFETY.md): campus
  safety, disability services, counseling, personal-information updates, and veterans services.
