## How to contact me

Use the course community as your first mode of contact. Post general questions in the class
channel so everyone benefits; send a direct message for private matters. Institutional email is
also appropriate for course matters, but please do not use my Gmail account. If I do not respond
within the stated response time, please send a follow-up message.

Phone contact is limited. I generally do not answer unknown numbers, the office phone does not
receive texts, and voicemail transcription may garble details. Use the course community or
institutional email for the quickest, clearest response. If scheduled office hours do not work,
send two or three possible meeting times and I will try to schedule one.

## When something feels urgent

In academia, unlike medical emergencies or other crises, most situations do not require immediate
attention, even those that may seem urgent. If you face a challenge such as a technical issue
during an exam:

* **Document the problem**: If you encounter difficulties with Blackboard, take screenshots and
  note any error messages.
* **Contact me**: Send a direct message through the course community or an institutional email
  explaining the issue.
* **Await my response**: I will address the problem during normal working hours. My methods for
  resolving the issue, such as granting time extensions or resets, are designed to ensure that you
  are not disadvantaged. **Rest assured, I will assist you as soon as I'm available.**
