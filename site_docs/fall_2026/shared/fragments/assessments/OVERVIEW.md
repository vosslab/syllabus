## Assessment types

An **assessment** is graded work that shows a student's understanding and ability to apply course
material. The guide below defines the assessment terms used across my courses; a course may not use
every type. Each course point plan gives the exact assessments and points for the semester.

**Assignments.** Assignments are outside-class practice, usually assigned weekly and worth 5-15
points. Each attempt is timed, but students may retry without a set limit, work with classmates,
and ask for help.

**Quizzes.** Quizzes check students' understanding of recent material. Each quiz begins with an
individual 20-point attempt of about 30 minutes, followed by an optional group attempt during class.

**Exams.** Exams are individual, cumulative assessments given during a scheduled midterm or final
exam period. An exam is usually worth about 100 points.

### Memorization is not learning

Remembering some course content may be necessary, but I am much more interested in whether students
can use scientific knowledge to solve a problem. The assessment types below focus on the process of
science: thinking, reasoning, analyzing, and communicating, rather than simply regurgitating facts.

The detailed sections after this overview describe only the assessment types used in each course.
