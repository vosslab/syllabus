## About your instructor

I studied mathematics and physics before earning a Ph.D. in molecular biophysics and biochemistry
from Yale University. Biology, quantitative reasoning, and programming all shape how I teach.

My grant-supported Open Educational Resources work creates free, dynamic biochemistry and genetics
problems shared through [Biology Problems](https://biologyproblems.org) and
[LibreTexts](https://libretexts.org): materials designed to be better for learning, not merely free.
Earlier, I developed cryo-EM software and studied protein geometry; outside academia, I build Lego
with [NILTC](https://www.niltc.org).
