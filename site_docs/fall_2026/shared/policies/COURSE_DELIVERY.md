## Online course tools

* **Zoom:** We will be participating in lectures via Zoom Video Conferencing software.
* **Blackboard:** Course information, announcements, private links, and scores in the gradebook will
  be available on Blackboard. Assignments, quizzes, and exams are not completed there.
* **Assessment platform:** The current course information will identify where assignments, quizzes,
  and online exams are completed before graded work begins.
* **Google Forms:** Some course forms may use Google Forms; this requires a Gmail account.
* **Google Slides:** Classroom presentations will be made using Google Slides; this requires a
  Gmail account.
* **Discord:** Course communication will take place over a Discord server; you will need to sign up.

## Course formats

### Face-to-face

Face-to-face courses will meet according to the scheduled time in the Wabash or Auditorium
Buildings in downtown Chicago, or on the Roosevelt campus in Schaumburg. Always inform your
professor when you cannot attend a class session. Please allow enough time to get to your courses
via the elevator or stairs in the Wabash and Auditorium buildings. Some face-to-face classes may
include an online Zoom component instead of meeting in person on all scheduled dates. To confirm
when Zoom meetings occur, you must check the course syllabus.

### Online

Online courses are web-based and asynchronous, with no set or required meeting days or times.
Blackboard provides course information and the gradebook. Learning activities and assessments use
the tools identified in the current course information. Asynchronous methods may include discussion
boards, readings, viewing videos, email exchanges, or assignments. As with all classes, there are
deadlines for assignments.

### Hybrid, including Zoom remote

Hybrid courses include a combination of online and in-person activities. In a hybrid learning
environment, the online and in-person components of the course should integrate in a cohesive
manner. The activities completed online complement those undertaken in the classroom. It is
important that students know when classes will meet in person and when the content will be
delivered online. The hybrid modality is good for flipping the classroom, where content is
delivered online and discussion or hands-on engaging activities take place in the classroom.
