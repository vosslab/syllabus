# Course format and online tools

## Changes to the syllabus

As the instructor, I reserve the right to modify any aspect of the course at any time. This
includes, but is not limited to, assignments, point values, due dates, schedules, course topics,
policies, formats, and procedures. My authority to make changes is not limited to a specific list
of reasons.

I will communicate syllabus changes through RU email and Blackboard and will also announce them in
class. It is your responsibility to attend class and regularly check your RU email and Blackboard
for updates or modifications.

## Online course tools

* **Blackboard:** Course information, announcements, private links, and scores in the gradebook will
  be available on Blackboard. Assignments, quizzes, and exams are not completed there.
* **Assessment platform:** The current course information will identify where assignments, quizzes,
  and online exams are completed before graded work begins.
* **Google Forms:** Some course forms may use Google Forms; this requires a Gmail account.
* **Discord:** Course communication will take place over a Discord server; you will need to sign up.

## Course format

The Course information page identifies this course's meeting format, locations, scheduled time,
and use of class time.
