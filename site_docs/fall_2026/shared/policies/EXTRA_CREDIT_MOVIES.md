# Approved science movies

These movies are approved choices for the science-movie extra-credit category. You may ask the
instructor to approve another movie. Older films can offer perspectives that differ from current
mainstream movies. Television series are allowed in some cases but are not recommended because one
or two episodes may not complete a satisfying story arc.

## Not approved

Choose an alternative to these movies:

- Outbreak (1995); watch Contagion instead.
- Mission to Mars (2000); watch The Martian instead.
- Weird Science (1985); watch Real Genius instead.
- Jurassic World (2015); watch Jurassic Park instead.
