# What students and instructors can expect

## Student expectations

* Regularly check Roosevelt Mail and Blackboard for any updates or important information related to the course.
  * Please check your RU email at least once per business day and the day before class meetings to stay updated.
  * Visit the Course Blackboard Site three times per week to stay up-to-date with course materials and announcements.
* Expect to spend about 3 hours on coursework and studying for every credit hour of class.
  * Review lecture notes regularly to reinforce learning and stay current with the course content.
  * Engage with peers through study groups or discussions outside of class to enhance understanding.
* Feel free to use my office hours or email me if you need help or have questions. **I am here to support you.**
* Submit assignments on time to keep pace with the course.
* Assignments are expected to be submitted on time; valid reasons are required for late submissions.

## Instructor expectations

* I will plan and prepare thoroughly for all course activities to ensure a structured and productive learning experience.
* I will keep the course moving at a pace that is suitable for the class, ensuring the opportunity to understand the material.
* You will have ample time to complete assignments, projects, and exams, with clear deadlines provided.
* I will be clear and responsive during lectures and labs, making sure that concepts are understandable and questions are addressed.
* I am committed to working with each of you to meet your needs and goals for the course, and I encourage open communication.
* Limited PREVIEW versions of lecture slides will be available on Blackboard before class to help you prepare.
* Full versions of lecture slides will be available on Blackboard within 36 hours after class for your review.
* I will follow the response time listed in [Contacting Dr. Voss](../INSTRUCTOR_INFORMATION.md).
* I will provide feedback on assignments and exams to support your learning and improvement.
* I will promote a respectful and inclusive classroom environment where all students feel valued and heard.

## Classroom expectations

* Attend class
  * Assume every class is important, and there is no need to ask, "Are we doing anything important today?"
  * Lectures will not be repeated privately, but you are welcome to review materials and ask questions.
* Arrive to class on time and come prepared for all course activities and lab exercises.
* Arriving on time and staying for the full class shows respect for the learning process and your classmates.
* Participate actively in discussions to help create a dynamic and engaging class environment.
  * Respect the opinions of others during discussions, even when they differ from your own.
