# Dropping or withdrawing from class

## Withdrawal from class

You may withdraw from a course and receive a grade of "W" by submitting an official request to
the Office of the Registrar by the published deadline. The current Fall 2026 deadline appears on
[Important dates and deadlines](../IMPORTANT_DATES.md).

Stopping attendance or telling only the instructor, advisor, or another university employee does
not count as an official withdrawal. If you do not withdraw officially, you may receive a failing
grade and remain responsible for tuition. Students receiving financial aid must also consult a
financial aid counselor. International students and student athletes may need additional approval.

See Roosevelt University's current
[course and credit policies](https://catalog.roosevelt.edu/undergraduate/policies/course-and-credit/)
and [registration information](https://www.roosevelt.edu/current-students/academics/register-classes)
for official instructions.

## Tuition refund schedule

If you are considering dropping this class, be aware that the amount of tuition refunded depends
on when you officially drop the class. The standard Fall and Spring schedule is:

* 100% refund: before the first day of week 2
* 50% refund: before the first day of week 3
* 25% refund: before the first day of week 4
* 0% refund: on or after the first day of week 4

Exact Fall 2026 dates appear on [Important dates and deadlines](../IMPORTANT_DATES.md). Roosevelt's
current refund policy also explains that undergraduate students who drop a class but remain
enrolled full-time do not receive a tuition refund or credit for the dropped course. Review the
[official tuition refund information](https://www.roosevelt.edu/current-students/finances/refunds)
and contact the Office of the Registrar before making a decision.

## Late withdrawal policy

A student facing an extraordinary non-academic emergency after the withdrawal deadline may submit
a Petition for Late Withdrawal. Roosevelt University currently allows this request within one year
of course completion. Submitting a petition does not guarantee approval.

See the Dean of Students'
[current student resources](https://www.roosevelt.edu/student-affairs/resources) for the petition
and instructions.
