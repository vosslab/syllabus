# Class expectations

## Hybrid participation

Both campus sections participate in one videoconferenced class. Discussion, presentations, and
peer feedback are central course activities. Students should arrive prepared to contribute and
should notify the instructor promptly about barriers to participating across campuses.

## Project and presentation work

Project work must use credible sources, distinguish evidence from speculation, and identify the
contributions of collaborators. Presentation deadlines and submission instructions are maintained
in Blackboard.

## Communication

Use Blackboard or Roosevelt email for course communication. Secure meeting links and course
invitations are published only in Blackboard.
