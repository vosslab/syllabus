# Class expectations

## Attendance and participation

Class meetings emphasize problem solving, discussion, and collaborative analysis. Students should
arrive prepared, participate respectfully, and complete the corresponding laboratory registration
and work required for their section.

## Collaborative work

Collaboration is encouraged when an activity identifies group work. Every student remains
responsible for understanding the submitted reasoning and for following the stated rules for
individual quizzes, exams, and assignments.

## Communication

Use Blackboard or Roosevelt email for course communication. Secure meeting links and course
invitations are published only in Blackboard.
