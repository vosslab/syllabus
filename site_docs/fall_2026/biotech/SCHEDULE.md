presentations, discussions, and other scheduled activities; **Work due** lists out-of-class
assignments due by that date.

Numbered online assignments begin in Week 3; Assignments 0 and 1 are both due that week.
Shark Tank slides are due six hours before class. Students complete peer-evaluation forms for
the individual and group presentations and the group-member contribution form for the group project.
