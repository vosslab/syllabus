# Class expectations

## Hybrid participation

The scheduled Wednesday meeting supports discussion, demonstrations, and questions. Additional
course work occurs asynchronously through Blackboard. Students are responsible for checking
Blackboard and Roosevelt email regularly and for completing work by its posted deadline.

## Data and technology

Students need reliable access to Blackboard and the software or browser tools identified with each
assignment. Statistical work must preserve the supplied data, document the analysis method, and
report results honestly. Contact the instructor promptly when a technical or accessibility barrier
affects an assignment.

## Communication

Use Blackboard or Roosevelt email for course communication. Secure meeting links and course
invitations are published only in Blackboard.
