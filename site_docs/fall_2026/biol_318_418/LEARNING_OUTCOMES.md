# What you will learn

## Roosevelt learning goals

This course supports effective communication, knowledge of discipline-focused content, and
awareness of social justice and civic engagement.

## Course outcomes

Students who complete the course will be able to:

1. Explain the risks of p-hacking and inappropriate statistical inference.
2. Use the central vocabulary of biostatistics accurately.
3. Select appropriate common hypothesis tests for biological data.
4. Calculate and interpret descriptive statistics.
5. Compare effective methods for presenting biological data.
6. Use statistical software to organize, analyze, and visualize data.
7. Interpret statistical results in the context of a biological question.
8. Communicate a data analysis and its limitations to a scientific audience.

## Applied objective

Students will complete a data-analysis project and present its biological question, method,
results, and limitations in an appropriate scientific format.
