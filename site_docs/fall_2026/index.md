# Fall 2026

The Fall 2026 session runs from August 31 through December 19, 2026.

## Courses

- [BIOL 318 and BIOL 418 - Biostatistics](biostats/index.md): hybrid course meeting Wednesdays at the
  Schaumburg campus.
- [BIOL 351 and BIOL 451 - General Genetics](genetics/index.md): face-to-face lecture meeting Tuesdays at
  the Schaumburg campus.
- [BIOL 480 - Applications of Biotechnology](biotech/index.md): hybrid course meeting Thursdays
  between the Chicago and Schaumburg campuses.
