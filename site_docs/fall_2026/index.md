# Fall 2026

The Fall 2026 session runs from August 31 through December 19, 2026.

**Accessibility note:** This online syllabus uses
[Atkinson Hyperlegible Next](https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/), a typeface designed by
the Braille Institute to make similar letters and numbers easier to distinguish, especially for
readers with low vision.
