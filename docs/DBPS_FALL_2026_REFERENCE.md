Department of Biological and Physical Sciences. The source message was supplied with the label
`DBPS Update 2025-08-18`, while its semester dates are for Fall 2026. The exact source remains in

site, PDF syllabi, or DOCX syllabi.

## Before classes begin

- Check Course Finder to confirm each course's current details.
- Post syllabi and general course information to Blackboard by Sunday, August 30, 2026.

## Blackboard and technology

Post course materials to Blackboard. Integrate ancillary textbook or digital-material access with
Blackboard when possible. Use the Blackboard help pages and bookstore guidance when setting up
publisher materials.

For immediate classroom technology problems, follow the instructions posted in the room or call
(312) 341-4357 and select option 3. For other technical support, search the knowledge base or file a
ticket through the [Roosevelt Help Desk](https://helpdesk.roosevelt.edu/).

For NetID login problems:

1. Reset the password through [My Roosevelt](https://my.roosevelt.edu/).
2. If the reset does not solve the problem, use the Login Issues option on the Roosevelt Help Desk
   site.
3. During evenings and weekends, use the Help Desk's support chat when it is available.

## Library resources

The [Roosevelt Library](https://www.roosevelt.edu/library) provides resources for finding,
analyzing, and using information. Library staff can assist students by chat and can host sessions
for courses and research projects.

## Syllabus submission

All credit-bearing courses require a syllabus using the current standardized university template,
including scheduled courses, independent study, and course-by-arrangement. Use the current
Policies and Resources material because older versions contain outdated university contacts.

- Post the course-specific syllabus and university policies to the course Blackboard site by
  Sunday, August 30, 2026.
- Submit the syllabus to the college Blackboard site by Sunday, September 6, 2026, following the
  college's separate instructions.
- Provide a Word document to support accessibility and college data collection.

## Office hours

Faculty may hold office hours in person or through Zoom. Online courses should provide online
office hours and a way for students to leave requests. Available in-person options include the
Learning Commons in AUD 1035, the STEM Center in AUD 835, faculty offices, and designated adjunct
workspaces.

## Rosters and attendance

Class rosters are available in RU Access and Blackboard. A student whose name does not appear on
the roster is not registered and should contact the Registrar's Office in WB 1M14 at
(312) 341-3535. Faculty should monitor and respond to Early Alert requests.

- During Week 1, students may add or drop courses and may be admitted from waitlists.
- During Week 2, adding a course requires advisor and instructor permission.
- Students should not be admitted after Week 2.
- Faculty must track and report attendance through at least Week 2.
- Meaningful participation, such as an introductory assignment or discussion post, establishes
  attendance in an online course.

## Disability accommodations

Disability Services uses AIM, Accessible Information Management, for accommodation letters.
Students request or renew accommodations through the system, and AIM sends the resulting letters
to instructors while copying the student. AIM-generated email addresses and a revised letter
format are expected.

Students who require extended exam time may use the testing center. Instructors submit proctoring
requests through Register Blast using the current university procedure.

- **Schaumburg questions:** Rebecca Berezin, rberezin@roosevelt.edu.
- **Chicago questions:** Nicole Yoon, nyoon@roosevelt.edu.

## Grades

Students who stop attending without withdrawing should be notified that they will receive an F.
Incomplete I grades require an I-Grade Contract submitted through the Dynamic Form in RU Access.
The form records the student, course, and remaining work and sends the completed agreement to the
student.

## Student support

### Tutoring

Tutoring may be available through:

- The Learning Commons in the Auditorium Building and SCH 517.
- The STEM Center in AUD 835.
- The Departmental Peer Tutoring Center in the Wabash Building.
- Online services.

Department tutoring focuses primarily on selected introductory and lower-level courses, especially
organic chemistry, rather than 300- or 400-level courses. Tutors may meet students in WB 716/717,
the STEM Center in AUD 835, or by Zoom appointment for Chicago and Schaumburg students. Course
assignments, availability polls, and finalized schedules vary by term and are distributed through
Blackboard.

When the department recruits peer tutors, faculty may recommend strong upper-level undergraduate
students. Advertise a specific opening only after the coordinator confirms the course need,
eligibility, application process, and term.

### English language support

The Office of International Programs connects eligible students with spoken and written English
support. Students may be referred after placement, identified through an Early Alert in Navigate,
or request help themselves.

### TRIO student support services

TRIO Student Support Services provides eligible STEM and non-STEM students with academic support,
individual advising, help navigating financial aid and campus resources, and a campus support
community. Review eligibility and apply through the
[TRIO Student Support Services](https://www.roosevelt.edu/trio-sss) page.

### Roosevelt Red Folder

The Roosevelt Red Folder helps faculty and staff recognize student distress, respond, make
referrals, and report concerns. Its reporting routes cover:

- Academic misconduct.
- Gender-based violence.
- Student conduct violations.
- Students experiencing a crisis or displaying concerning behavior.

## Laboratory support

Nathan La Porte is the department laboratory contact for laboratory courses, independent study,
and research. Students and faculty may contact him about laboratory access and planning, lab-coat
purchases, safety-document completion, and other laboratory support.

All faculty who teach or sponsor students in laboratories must complete laboratory safety training
at least annually. Part-time faculty are eligible for mandatory-duty pay for required safety
training.

## Faculty absences

For a planned or unexpected absence:

1. Notify rseiser@roosevelt.edu and cshp@roosevelt.edu. Include the class time and location if a
   sign must be posted.
2. For a planned absence, attempt to arrange a substitute, exchange classes with another
   instructor, hold a short-term Zoom meeting, or provide a recorded lesson in Blackboard.
3. Give a substitute a written summary of the material and activities to cover.
4. When no substitute is available, notify students by email and Blackboard and state the work they
   should complete.

Zoom may address a short-term absence, but a scheduled in-person course remains an in-person course
and cannot be converted into a long-term remote course.

## Course evaluations

Online course evaluations open approximately two weeks before final exams and close the night
before exams begin. Faculty should remind students to participate and may provide class time for
completion. Any course incentive should be announced clearly and applied consistently.

## Department operations

- Full-time faculty are expected to attend department-wide faculty meetings and College Council
  meetings unless a class conflict or approved absence prevents attendance.
- Part-time faculty are welcome but are not normally required to attend faculty meetings and
  departmental events.
- Faculty service, committee work, and departmental events may require Common Hour participation.
- Shared printers and office supplies are available in the Wabash Building and SCH 600. Faculty
  should conserve resources and generally stay below 50 copies per student per semester.
- Full-time faculty should confirm annual teaching loads and approved course releases with the
  department chair. Part-time faculty may teach up to two courses per semester or four per academic
  year.
