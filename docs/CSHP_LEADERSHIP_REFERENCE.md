# CSHP leadership reference

Confirmed by Neil R. Voss on August 31, 2026. This tracked, public-safe document supports syllabus
review and departmental reference. It is not a student-facing syllabus source and is not included
in the MkDocs site, PDF syllabi, or DOCX syllabi.

## Checklist interpretation

- The department checklist requests department chair information and program director information
  only when a program director is applicable.
- Biological, Physical and Health Sciences is no longer a current department. It split into the
  Department of Biological and Physical Sciences (BPS) and the Department of Health Sciences (HS).
- Robert Seiser, PhD, is the applicable department chair for the Fall 2026 Biology courses.
- No Allied Health Director or Pre-Professional Program Director is part of the current structure.
- No listed program director overlaps BIOL 318/418, BIOL 351/451, or BIOL 480.
- College deans, associate deans, unrelated directors, academic advisors, and the Health
  Professions Program Coordinator are not checklist evidence for these courses.

## Role clarifications

### Allied health

There is no position explicitly titled Allied Health Director. Allied-health programs fall within
the college leadership and their individual program structures.

### Pre-professional programs

There is no standalone Pre-Professional Program Director. Pre-health professional preparation is a
designation rather than a major, minor, or concentration. Students use academic advising and the
Health Professions Program Coordinator for guidance.
