this report identifies it by path rather than linking to a target that will not exist on GitHub.

## Covered components

The current syllabi already provide:

- Roosevelt identification; instructor name; office locations; email; telephone; office hours;
  response expectations; and other contact methods.
- Course formats, meeting times, locations, credit hours, session dates, prerequisites, and catalog
  descriptions.
- Weekly schedules and schedule-change disclaimers.
- Roosevelt learning goals and course learning outcomes.
- Genetics and Biotechnology assignments, points, and weights.
- AI, grading, incomplete-grade, attendance, participation, civility, device, late-work,
  missed-exam, extra-credit, and withdrawal policies.
- The Fall 2026 withdrawal deadline through the generated Important Dates content.
- Student services and university resources, including Schaumburg resources.
- DOCX output, semantic headings, structured tables, descriptive links, and accessible color
  handling.
- Assignment submission expectations and Blackboard as the authoritative submission and grade
  record.

## Accessibility applicability

Captioned videos and image alt text are currently not applicable to the syllabus body because it
does not embed instructional videos or content images. Recheck those rubric items whenever media is
added.

## Main finding

The largest substantive course-specific gap is the missing BIOL 318/418 point plan. The remaining
main shared gaps are concrete feedback timing and student acknowledgment of policies and resources.
The department chair and explicit textbook and technology statements are now covered. Applicable
program-director coverage remains unconfirmed for each course.
