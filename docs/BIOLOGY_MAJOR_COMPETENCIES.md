# Biology major competency reference

## Purpose

The undergraduate Biology curricula require the major electives, taken collectively, to cover six
disciplinary competency areas. These competencies come from the Vision and Change framework used
for biology curriculum planning. They are distinct from Roosevelt CORE general-education
attributes.

This tracked reference supports syllabus maintenance and department review. It is outside the
`site_docs/` MkDocs source tree and is not included in the public course website or generated
student syllabi. Public course-information tables list only the competency labels applicable to
each course.

## Six competency areas

1. **Applying the Process of Science** - Formulate questions and hypotheses, design or evaluate
   investigations, analyze evidence, and draw supported conclusions.
2. **Quantitative Reasoning** - Use mathematics, statistics, and data analysis to investigate and
   interpret biological questions.
3. **Modeling and Simulation** - Construct, use, and evaluate models or simulations of biological
   systems and processes.
4. **Interdisciplinary Nature of Science** - Integrate biology with relevant ideas and methods from
   other scientific and technical disciplines.
5. **Communication and Collaboration** - Communicate scientific reasoning and evidence clearly and
   work productively with people within and across disciplines.
6. **Science and Society** - Evaluate relationships among biological science, ethics, policy,
   history, and society.
